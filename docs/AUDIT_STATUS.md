## P1 — economic correctness of the historical engine
- **done** — Resting stop persists across volume-capped bars (completes at later bar opens) instead of vanishing or being re-tested (`77338f0`).
- **done** — `available_at` uses a **computed** session calendar (`lib/market_calendar.py`) for any year, ET session close, early-close half-days; non-session dates roll forward (`77338f0`).
- **done** — Daily date contract: date-only daily bars at 00:00 UTC belong to that session (no UTC→NY back-shift) (`77338f0`).
- **done** — Future-independence tested across multiple symbols and symbol ordering; hash invariant to symbol order (`77338f0`, `tests/test_backtest_economics.py`).
- **done** — Metrics added: drawdown duration, avg/max exposure, avg win/loss; per-account `first_fill_timestamp`/`warmup_bars`; explicit warmup-comparability and no-OOS notes (`77338f0`).
- **partial** — Intrabar realism (gaps, simultaneous stop/target, partial fills): stop-first tie-break, entry-bar risk and resting stops modeled; full tick-accurate sequencing is not derivable from OHLC and stays declared as a limitation.
- **pending** — Warmup vs. evaluation window: disclosed via `first_fill_timestamp`, but a shared warmup/evaluation split for like-for-like benchmark comparison is not implemented.
- **done** — **Point-in-time universe** (`CsvPointInTimeUniverse`): the portfolio backtest reads index membership per date from a composition CSV (long `date,symbol` or wide `date,symbols`, forward-filled) via `ResearchConfig.universe_csv`, so it only holds names that were actually members on each date, and freezes the file's SHA256 into the result. `load_csv` now accepts dotted tickers (`BRK.B`) with path-traversal protection. Tested: membership/forward-fill, a late joiner is not held before its membership date, determinism. **Pending (data, not code)**: fetch the actual S&P 500 historical constituents + delisted prices (needs an external source; deferred with Tiingo).
- **done** — **Portfolio (cross-sectional) strategy layer**: `PortfolioStrategy`/`PortfolioTarget` contract (`Strategy` untouched), `RebalancePlanner` (atomic: one equity snapshot → full target → full deltas; sells first; buys scaled proportionally to available cash), backtester second decision path (once per batch, last session of the month, next-open execution), `xsmom_12_1_long_only` (preregistered 12-1, top decile, equal weight, monthly, long only, no brackets) and the `equal_weight_universe` benchmark, plus outputs: rebalances/holdings, one-way turnover, next-period **decile ladder**, excess over the equal-weight universe. Tests: symbol-order invariance (incl. scarce cash), no lookahead, determinism, planner scaling/turnover. Smoke on a 24-name FIXED universe showed the plumbing works but **no decile ladder** (losers ≈ winners next month) — i.e. no detectable momentum signal in that survivorship-biased sample; the +42% excess came from a 3-name concentration, not the factor. The combined xsmom + absolute-momentum ablation is implemented (`xsmom_12_1_abs_filter`, de-risks to cash when relative winners have negative absolute momentum; `tests/test_xsmom_abs_filter.py`). **Pending**: a point-in-time `Universe.members(as_of)` with hundreds of names (the real experiment) and quintiles for small universes.
- **done** — Operational sizing parity: `apps/risk_manager` now sizes to the allocation cap when `stop_loss_pct` is disabled (Settings accept `None` for stop/take-profit) instead of dividing by the stop. The live executor honours `require_protective_exits` (default True): with it False, a BUY without stop/take-profit submits as a plain order (for pure trend/momentum strategies), enforced consistently in `execution_safety.check_order` and the executor. Tested (`tests/test_no_bracket.py`).
- **partial** — Walk-forward **consistency report** implemented: `walk_forward_folds` scores the same fixed-parameter strategy over N contiguous sub-periods (per-window return/drawdown + aggregate mean/stdev/min/max/fraction-positive). **Pending**: walk-forward *optimization* (there is no fitting step), confidence intervals, dividends/splits/tax ledger, point-in-time universe / survivorship.
